# Methodology

The methodology is one page, on the docs site:
**[yotambraun.github.io/saylent/docs/methodology](https://yotambraun.github.io/saylent/docs/methodology)**

It is the whole document, not a summary: what is measured, where the questions
come from, how the engines and the answer surfaces are asked, how an answer is
judged (including the cross-family judge and the golden set), what is checked
on the cited pages, Sources & opportunities, what the site access checks know,
how fixes are produced, what is scored, which models are used, the limits, and
what sample data is published.

Two topics have their own page as well:

- **Answer surfaces** (Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot,
  read through your own SerpApi key; "not shown" answers):
  [docs/surfaces](https://yotambraun.github.io/saylent/docs/surfaces)
- **Sources & opportunities** (citation share, share of voice as counts,
  off-site opportunities, the domain-type rules):
  [docs/sources](https://yotambraun.github.io/saylent/docs/sources)

Every "see METHODOLOGY.md" in the source means that page. The source for it
lives in this repository at `website/content/docs/methodology.mdx`.
