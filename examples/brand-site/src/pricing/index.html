What counts as a check

A check is one thing Kestrel watches on a schedule: an HTTP endpoint, a TCP port, a cron heartbeat, an SSL certificate expiry, or a keyword that has to be present in a response. The four regions are not four checks. One endpoint watched from Frankfurt, Virginia, Singapore and São Paulo is a single check against your plan.

Seats are not counted and never will be. Adding the whole engineering team, the support lead and a contractor costs the same as monitoring alone, because charging by the person is a good way to end up with one exhausted person holding the pager.

Questions we get asked

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

No. Kestrel is billed monthly and you can stop at the end of any month. We would rather earn the next month than lock in the next year.

What happens when I pass my check limit?

Nothing breaks. Kestrel keeps running every existing check and asks you to move up a plan before adding more. We do not turn off monitoring for a billing reason.

Do you charge for SMS?

SMS to the countries listed in the docs is included on Flight and Squadron. There is no per-message charge and no credit bundle to top up.

Can I use my own domain for a status page?

Yes, on Flight and Squadron. Point a CNAME at Kestrel and the certificate is issued for you, usually within two minutes.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Checks stop, the status page returns a plain notice, and the incident history stays available for export for 60 days. After that it is deleted.
