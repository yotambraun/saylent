About

We are a small team that got tired of learning about outages from customers.

Kestrel was started by Dana Whitlock and Ravi Tolman after a night spent on a call where nobody could agree on whether the service was actually down. Two dashboards said yes, one said no, and the only person who could read the alerting rules was asleep. We wanted a tool that answered that one question quickly and then got out of the way.

The team works remotely, with a small office in Marrowfield where we meet twice a month. We take turns on our own on call rotation, which is the fastest way we know to notice when an alert is annoying rather than useful.

We are funded by the people who pay for it. There is no growth target handed to us by anyone else, which is why the free plan is a plan rather than a trial, and why we bill monthly.

If you want to talk to a person about monitoring, even if you end up choosing something else, write to hello@kestrel.example and one of us will answer.
