Saylent is one shape of tool in a category that also has well-funded hosted
products. This page states what is structurally different, and separately
what has been reported about those products' pricing, with a source and a
date for every number, plus the vendor's own pricing page linked where one
exists publicly. It does not rate any product's quality - see [the
methodology](/docs/methodology) for what Saylent itself does and does not
claim.

## What is structurally different

| | Saylent | Hosted tools (Profound, Peec, Otterly, Scrunch, AthenaHQ, Semrush AI Toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar, HubSpot AEO Grader) |
|---|---|---|

| Runs on your own provider keys | Yes - every dollar goes to OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Perplexity directly, never through us ([What it costs](/docs/costs)) | No - usage runs on the vendor's own infrastructure and account, priced into their subscription |
| Self-hostable | Yes - CLI on your machine, or the full app on your own server, Docker, or your own Vercel/Supabase project ([Self-host](/docs/self-host)) | No - hosted SaaS only |

| Cross-family judge (self-preference control) | Yes when two provider keys are set - one family's answer is judged by the other; the report states `judge_mode` either way ([Methodology](/docs/methodology)) | Not published |

| Entry price | $0 - open source, self-hosted | See the table below - each vendor's reported starting price, sourced |

## What the hosted tools do that Saylent does not (yet)

This is the honest gap, not a hedge:

- **Continuous historical tracking at scale.** Saylent's `verify` command
  re-runs the frozen question set and diffs against one baseline run - a
  before/after, not a running multi-week or multi-month trend line across
  many brands.
- **Alerts.** Saylent has two *optional*, off-by-default scheduled jobs
  (`FLAG_SCHEDULED_RUNS`: a weekly verify, a monthly audit) on a self-hosted
  deployment - not real-time or threshold-based alerting.
- **Multi-tenant dashboards.** The self-hosted app is built for one operator
  running it for their own brand(s), not a shared workspace serving many
  client accounts from one login.
- **Team seats and roles.** There is no multi-seat team plan or per-seat
  role model.
- **Vendor support.** A hosted subscription buys a support relationship;
  an open-source project's support is public GitHub issues and (where
  enabled) Discussions.

## Why this page exists

Every paid entry tier above is roughly $29-300/mo, and several already offer a free
one-shot check as their own lead-generation ([Ahrefs' free AI visibility
checker](https://ahrefs.com/ai-visibility-checker) is a "no signup" example).
An "open-source alternative to an expensive incumbent" story does not
describe this category the way it describes, say, Plausible vs Google
Analytics - Saylent's reason to exist is different: your own keys, your own
data, self-hostable, and the receipts behind every score, not a lower price
for the same closed product.
