Saylent asks real buyer questions to real AI assistants - ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini and Perplexity, through their official APIs - records every answer
verbatim, and tells you whether your brand was recommended, mentioned or
absent, which rival was named instead, which pages the assistants cited, and
what to fix. It runs on your own provider keys, on your own machine or your
own infrastructure. Nothing is sent through us and nothing is stored by us.

## Change what it asks and which models judge

The questions, the sample count, the answer engines, the judge and drafter
models, and which stages run at all are every one of them yours to set - from
a flag, an environment variable, a `saylent.config.json`, or the app's own
Questions page. [Configuration reference](/docs/configuration) has the
complete config file, the default for every field, and one table saying where
each control lives on each surface.
