Saylent asks real buyer questions to real AI assistants - ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini and Perplexity, through their official APIs - records every answer
verbatim, and tells you whether your brand was recommended, mentioned or
absent, which rival was named instead, which pages the assistants cited, and
what to fix. It runs on your own provider keys, on your own machine or your
own infrastructure. Nothing is sent through us and nothing is stored by us.

These docs are one story in six parts, in the order people actually need them.
